THE FORGOTTEN NAME By CL HARRIS Ilyra Venn is dead, and her life will not stop working. Rent still clears. A child is still collected from school. An old man still receives care in her name—and no one who benefits from any of it can quite say who she was. Sere Arden examines the dead for a living. She tests evidence, exposes contradiction, and certifies what a record can bear. It is careful work, built on the conviction that what happened leaves something behind. But Ilyra Venn's case refuses to behave like evidence should. A damaged record. A contradiction where none should exist. Obligations that continue without anyone remembering the woman who created them. And everywhere Sere looks, the same impossible absence: Ilyra's life has left consequences behind, while Ilyra herself is disappearing from the world that still depends on them. Then the case begins reaching into Sere's own life. The closer she comes to understanding what happened to Ilyra, the harder it becomes to trust the certainties on which she has built her work—and herself. Some things are forgotten because they no longer matter. Sere is beginning to suspect Ilyra Venn was erased because she still does. Discover more about The Forgotten Name at ForgottenName.com.
